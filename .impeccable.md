# Design context — Kina Wige

Captured from the owner's own direction across the project, not inferred from
code. Edit freely; design skills read this first.

## Who it is for

**The app** is for children aged 3–6 in Rwanda. Pre-literate. Often on a shared
phone, often offline, often a low-end Android device.

**The marketing site** is for their **parents** — adults deciding whether to
trust this with their child. Second audience: schools, districts and funders.
Confirmed by the owner: *parents first, funders second.*

## What the parent is trying to do

1. Understand in ten seconds what the app is and whether it is for their child.
2. Find out if it costs money, needs internet, or takes their data.
3. See what their child will actually learn.
4. Install it.

## Brand personality

From `src/index.css` and `CLAUDE.md`, the app's system is already opinionated:

- **Flat, chunky, high-contrast.** Every raised surface carries a SOLID bottom
  shadow. **No blurs. No gradients.** Pressing drops it 4–6px.
- Warm cream paper ground, deep forest green as the dominant, sun yellow as the
  sharp accent.
- Confident and plain-spoken, never cute-for-cute's-sake and never corporate.

The site must feel like the same hand made both, while being unmistakably an
adult's page — the child's world is inside the app, not on the website.

## Non-negotiables that shape design

- **Trilingual: KN / EN / FR.** Kinyarwanda is first, not a translation.
- **§18 forbids "generic Africa"** — nothing that could be anywhere on the
  continent. A Rwandan parent must recognise their own country.
- **No fabricated proof.** No invented testimonials, awards, download counts or
  impact numbers. No pilot has run yet. The credibility comes from what is
  verifiably true: the curriculum, the offline guarantee, the privacy promise.
- **No dead links.** The app is not on either app store yet; badges stay
  dormant until the listings exist.

## The one thing to remember

*"We can tell you exactly what your child is learning, and we never take
anything from you."*

Every competitor optimises for time-in-app. Kina Wige caps a session at 12
minutes, stores nothing off-device, and can name the skill behind every screen.
That is the whole position.
